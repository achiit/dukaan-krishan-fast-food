Meet Krishan

I started with one tawa and a small cart near the old sabji mandi. My elder brother taught me how to get the noodles just right: high flame, quick toss, no shortcuts.

Today people from all over Kalanwali stop by after shopping at the mandi. Some of them have been eating here since their school days, and now they bring their kids.

The recipe has stayed the same. Fresh vegetables every morning, and the food goes from the tawa straight to your plate.

  • Cooked fresh after you order
  • Fresh vegetables bought daily from the mandi
  • Fair prices for everyone
  • Clean hands, clean tawa
